      Controls and scoring

      ↑Main engine: pushes the way the nose points
      ↓Retro engine: pushes the opposite way
      ← →Rotate (the spin keeps going until you stop it)
      SStabilize: stops the rotation

      HThis help

      Landing

      Touch down slower than 1.5 and within 20° of upright. The dotted line shows where you are heading: green means a safe touchdown speed, red means a crash.

      Scoring

      • Gates: faster than par earns a speed bonus; reaching the next gate before the combo bar runs out raises the multiplier up to x3.
      • Gyro fault: the faster you stop the spin, the better the grade.
      • Bonus beacon: worth more the sooner you grab it.
      • Landing: soft, upright and centred on the pad scores extra.
      • Finish: spare time and fuel add bonuses. The total decides your stars.
